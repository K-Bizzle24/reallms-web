FROM BLANK PAGE TO FIRST EXCHANGE

It's a conversation.
Not a one-shot question.

  1. Come on in.

    Open REALLMS Chat and choose Continue with IU Login. Choose a model to get started.

  2. Give it something to work with.

    Ask your question, explain your goal, or upload a document. A little context goes further than a complicated prompt.

  3. Pull on a thread.

    Ask a follow-up. Challenge an assumption. Check important claims against your sources. You're still the expert on your work.

02 / MAKE IT YOURS API Access

Less copy-paste.
More possibility.

Put a model in your editor. Make a request from a script. Connect it to a research pipeline. An API key lets your tools talk to REALLMS.

  1. Start with your project.

    Sign in to RTProjects, IU's resource-access portal.

    Check with your PI first. If they already have a project, coordinate being added and requesting a REALLMS allocation if one is missing.

    Need a new project? Create one with a brief description of your research or coursework. Students need their PI's username.

  2. Get a REALLMS allocation.

    An allocation gives your project access to the service. Request one if needed, then wait for approval before generating a key.

    Joining an approved project? Ask to be added to both the project and its allocation.

  3. Your key. Just for you.

    Open the approved allocation's page and generate your own API key. Every collaborator needs an individual key. Don't share it or commit it to code.

  4. Pick a tool. Connect the dots.

    Start with a direct request, or connect an assistant you already use. The recipes below show the configuration and how to check your first response.
